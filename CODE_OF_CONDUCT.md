# Code of Conduct

MAIPL is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints and
experience, and focus criticism on ideas rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to Yue Su at
[yue.su@dal.ca](mailto:yue.su@dal.ca). Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
